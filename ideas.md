# Ohora Shopify Operations Portfolio — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Black-box Commerce Lab
Very Brief Intro: A dark, high-contrast operator portfolio inspired by Shopify Admin, premium SaaS dashboards, and editorial case studies. Acid-lime signals measurable growth while dense black space creates focus and authority.
Probability: 0.073

### Theme Name: Paper Commerce Journal
Very Brief Intro: A warm, light editorial portfolio using cream paper, ink typography, and restrained red annotations. It would feel like a printed operating manual translated into a modern web experience.
Probability: 0.041

### Theme Name: Nordic Storefront Index
Very Brief Intro: A cool white and cobalt system with precise grids, catalog-like cards, and quiet Swiss typography. The result would be highly legible, practical, and more understated than the reference.
Probability: 0.086

## Chosen approach: Black-box Commerce Lab

### Design Movement
Neo-editorial systems design: the visual language of a high-performing commerce control room, expressed with the discipline of a magazine case study and the clarity of a product analytics dashboard.

### Core Principles
1. **Evidence before decoration.** KPIs, operating stages, and before/after logic should carry the visual hierarchy.
2. **Dark field, bright signal.** The page stays nearly black and uses one ownable green accent for decisions, action, and proof.
3. **Editorial asymmetry.** Sections use offset columns, rails, and oversized numbering instead of repetitive centered blocks.
4. **Operational clarity.** Every visual should help a recruiter understand what was operated, what broke, what changed, and why it mattered.

### Color Philosophy
A near-black background creates the feeling of a calm operations room rather than a loud marketing site. A high-energy acid green is reserved for measurable outcomes, active states, links, and the “growth signal.” Soft gray text keeps long-form case-study content readable, while thin graphite borders recreate the structure of an admin interface without looking like a generic dashboard.

### Layout Paradigm
A sticky left rail on desktop anchors the narrative while the content advances through wide, offset modules. The hero is split between a large statement and a compact operating system panel. Case studies alternate between narrative blocks and KPI evidence. On mobile, the rail becomes a compact horizontal utility bar and the asymmetric composition collapses into a single reading column.

### Signature Elements
- A small green “live system” pulse beside section labels and the portfolio wordmark.
- Oversized lime section numbers used as navigation anchors and visual punctuation.
- Thin ruled frames with monospaced metadata labels, inspired by Shopify Admin exports and experiment logs.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like inspecting a system: direct, fast, and informative. Hovering a case row reveals its operating scope; expandable details expose Situation → Diagnosis → Action → Impact; nav links smoothly jump to the corresponding system module. Motion is subtle and functional, never ornamental.

### Animation
Use short opacity-and-translate entrances with 180–260ms easing. KPI values can gently rise into place on first view, while case rows shift 4px on hover and reveal an accent line. Avoid looping animations except for the tiny live-system pulse. Respect reduced-motion preferences by disabling transforms and keeping focus states visible.

### Typography System
Use **Space Grotesk** for headlines and key numeric proof points, with **IBM Plex Mono** for metadata, labels, and navigation. Body copy uses **DM Sans** for warmth and readability. Headline hierarchy is large, compact, and sentence-case; labels are uppercase with generous tracking; KPI numerals are heavy and tight.

### Brand Essence
**Shopify + eCommerce + CRO + lifecycle operator for teams that want the store to perform, not merely stay updated.**
Personality: **analytical, hands-on, commercially-minded**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and evidence-led. CTAs sound like an invitation to inspect the operating model, not a generic sales pitch. Microcopy is concise, specific, and practical.

Example lines:
- “I operate the storefront as a revenue system.”
- “Find the friction. Fix the flow. Measure the lift.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark is a compact lowercase `ohora / ops` lockup with a green square pulse mark: a simple open bracket containing a dot, suggesting a system being monitored in real time. The mark appears in the header and can be reused as the favicon treatment.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Green — #2DFFB3.** It is vivid enough to own the page, but cooler and more technical than a conventional ecommerce green. Use it sparingly so every appearance feels like a meaningful operating signal.

## Content guardrails

The portfolio will use the supplied assignment brainstorm as the source of truth. It will present store/project names, responsibilities, tools, and metrics as provided, while framing the Ohora Germany section explicitly as a proposed operating approach rather than an audit or claim about Ohora’s current performance. No customer reviews, ratings, or testimonials will be invented.

## Page narrative

WHO I AM → WHAT I’VE OPERATED → HOW I OPERATE → PROBLEMS I’VE SOLVED → TOOLS → HOW I WOULD OPERATE OHORA → WHY I’M A FIT.

The page should feel consumable in 60–90 seconds for a recruiter and expandable into a deeper operating conversation for a hiring manager.

## Style Decisions

- Follow the reference portfolio’s dark canvas, electric green accent, oversized typography, KPI strip, sticky navigation, and case-study-first storytelling.
- Differentiate the new portfolio through a Shopify operations lens: system map, funnel stages, issue-resolution cards, and a 0–90 day Ohora operating plan.
- Avoid generic company-logo walls, excessive rounded cards, purple gradients, and decorative visuals that do not explain the work.

## Style Decisions

- Signal Green remains reserved for proof, action, active navigation, key numerals, and one intentional page-level inversion.
- Desktop structure now carries an operating-console rail with live status, section markers, and OPS.LOG labeling.
- Case and funnel modules use operating-record, export, and issue-log labels so visuals explain the work rather than acting as decoration.
